Welcome to What Should I Have For Dinner Tonight!

We're excited to help you solve one of life's most persistent questions: what should I have for dinner tonight?

## Our Mission

Our goal is to make dinner decisions easier. Whether you want to cook something delicious with ingredients you already have, get suggestions for grocery shopping, or find the perfect restaurant near you, we've got you covered.

## How It Works

1. **Cook at Home**: Use our recipe builder to find recipes based on what you have in your pantry, or get suggestions with a shopping list.

2. **Order Out**: Tell us your mood, dietary preferences, and location, and we'll help you find great restaurants nearby.

3. **Save Your History**: When you're signed in, we'll save your requests so you can easily revisit your favorite recipes and restaurants.

We're constantly working on new features:
